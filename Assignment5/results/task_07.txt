Task 7: Retrieve all students studying in Semester 5
Description: Filters students who are in semester 5 (Aarav Sharma, Priya Das, Arjun Patel).

Cypher Query:
MATCH (s:Student) WHERE s.semester = 5 RETURN s.studentId AS StudentID, s.name AS Name, s.semester AS Semester, s.email AS Email ORDER BY s.studentId;

Output (3 rows):
StudentID | Name         | Semester | Email         
----------+--------------+----------+---------------
S101      | Aarav Sharma | 5        | aarav@univ.edu
S102      | Priya Das    | 5        | priya@univ.edu
S105      | Arjun Patel  | 5        | arjun@univ.edu
