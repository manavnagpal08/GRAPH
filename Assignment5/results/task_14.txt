Task 14: Find all students enrolled in both Graph Analytics and another course
Description: Retrieves students enrolled in Graph Analytics who are also taking at least one other course.

Cypher Query:
MATCH (s:Student)-[:ENROLLED_IN]->(c1:Course {courseTitle: 'Graph Analytics'}), (s)-[:ENROLLED_IN]->(c2:Course) WHERE c1 <> c2 RETURN s.studentId AS StudentID, s.name AS StudentName, c1.courseTitle AS PrimaryCourse, c2.courseTitle AS OtherCourse ORDER BY s.studentId, c2.courseTitle;

Output (4 rows):
StudentID | StudentName  | PrimaryCourse   | OtherCourse

S101      | Aarav Sharma | Graph Analytics | NoSQL Databases
